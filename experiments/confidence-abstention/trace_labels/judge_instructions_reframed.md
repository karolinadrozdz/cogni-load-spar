reworded. Each item carries its own `phase2_instruction` field; it replaces the rubric's description of the
Phase-2 instruction for that item. All items here ended in abstention (`final_answer` = 5).
`instruction_license` means abstaining by citing that item's instruction (e.g. "if you are not sure, choose 5 to
skip") with no other grounds. Everything else in the rubric applies unchanged.
